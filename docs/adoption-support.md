# Adoption Support and Consultation

Authrim is an open-source identity platform.

Built around OIDC, OAuth 2.0, SAML, and SCIM, Authrim is being developed to support a wide range of use cases, including multitenancy and federation.

To improve the product and gather operational knowledge, we accept consultation requests from organizations considering adoption.

## Organizations We Welcome

Authrim is not a product for a single industry.

For example, we are particularly interested in:

- SaaS and B2B service providers
- Companies building internal authentication or identity infrastructure
- MSPs, SIers, and platform providers
- Universities and research institutions
- Libraries and consortia
- Non-profit organizations and communities
- Organizations working on federation and distributed authentication

We also welcome any other use cases where Authrim may be a good fit.

## What We Can Support

- Authrim evaluation and PoC
- OIDC / OAuth 2.0 adoption
- SAML integration
- SCIM integration
- Federation architecture
- Multitenant design
- Operational architecture on Cloudflare
- Migration planning from existing IAM products
- Authorization and authentication architecture review

## How We Can Help

- Architecture review
- Initial setup support
- Design review
- Implementation approach consultation
- Feedback sessions
- Validation environment support

## What We Cannot Support

At this time, we do not provide:

- Contract development
- On-site support
- Operations outsourcing
- Support contracts with SLA commitments
- 24/7 monitoring and maintenance

## Feedback

Authrim grows by learning from real operational requirements and use cases.

When organizations consider adoption, we may ask for support within reasonable limits, including:

- Sharing usage scenarios and requirements
- Feedback on design and operational issues
- Sharing architecture examples when anonymization is possible
- Reporting bugs and improvement points

We do not ask for confidential information or personal data.

## Reference Use Cases

To support project improvement and adoption growth, we may ask for permission to feature your deployment as a case study.

Content can be published with your approval, and public scope can be adjusted.

The following formats are supported:

- Case studies with organization name and logo
- Case studies with only industry disclosed
- Case studies describing the technical architecture
- Fully anonymized case studies

Publishing a case study is optional.

## Current Policy

Authrim is not a bespoke development project for a single vendor. We are focused on building a long-term product.

Because of this, we may prioritize features and mechanisms that provide broad value over short-term, one-off requirements.

The time we can dedicate to adoption support and review is limited, so some requests may not be accepted depending on timing and content.

## Contact

Please share your intended use cases and current evaluation stage to the extent possible.

We also welcome inquiries even if you are unsure whether Authrim is the right fit.
